Saudi Basic Industries Corporation (“SABIC”) (2010-SA) today announced its financial results for the full 
year of 2025, reporting a free cash flow of SAR 7.2 billion [US$ 1.92 billion]—an increase of 17% compared to 
2024. 
 
 
2025 HIGHLIGHTS: 
 
• Revenue of SAR 116.53 billion [US$ 31.07 billion], a decrease of  1% Y-o-Y. 
• Adjusted2 EBITDA1 of SAR 17.88 billion [US 4.77 billion], a decrease of  15% Y-o-Y. 
• Adjusted2 EBIT3 of SAR 6.92 billion [US$ 1.84 billion], a decrease of 29% Y-o-Y. 
• Adjusted2 Net Income4 of SAR 2.07 billion [US$ 0.55 billion], a decreases of 65% Y-o-Y. 
• Adjusted2 Earnings Per Share4 of SAR 0.69 [US$ 0.18], a decreases of 65% Y-o-Y. 
• Net cash position of SAR 3.6 billion [US$ 0.96 billion] as of December 31, 2025, compared to a net cash 
position of SAR 3.0 billion [US$ 0.80 billion] as of December 31, 2024. 
 
ABDULRAHMAN AL-FAGEEH, CEO AND EXECUTIVE BOARD MEMBER:  
   
“2025 reflected a moderately improving macroeconomic landscape. Yet, production overcapacity persisted 
in the petrochemical industry, continuing to squeeze margins and depress utilization rates. Amid these 
conditions, SABIC remained focused on meeting its 2025 priorities. 
 
We achieved a total recordable incident rate of 0.07, the lowest in SABIC’s history. This represents a 22% 
year-over-year improvement in performance across the combined areas of environment, health, safety, 
and security. And we remain committed to delivering value to our shareholders, announcing the 
distribution of SAR 9.0 [US$ 2.4 billion] in dividends for the full year of 2025.  
 
SABIC continued to take decisive action to optimize its asset portfolio. As we announced earlier this year, 
two agreements have been signed to divest our European petrochemicals business and our engineering 
thermoplastics business in the Americas and Europe. Once closed, these transactions will reinforce the 
company’s long-term strategic positioning to create maximum value for its shareholders. Complementing 
these actions, our transformation journey delivered tangible results, realizing approximately 623 million 
dollars in recurring annual EBITDA support. This puts us well on the way to achieving our 2030 
transformation target. 
 
We also continued to position ourselves for selective, sustainable growth by taking final investment 
decisions on two projects. One project is an ethylene oxide catalyst plant in Saudi Arabia. Supported by the 
Shareek public-private partnership program, the catalyst plant is intended to advance the localization and 
security of our catalyst supply. The other project is a wholly owned engineering thermoplastics 
compounding plant in China. Both projects show how we are investing capital in a disciplined, value-
focused way. We also announced an expansion of our capacity to produce polyphenylene-ether-based 
specialty polymers. This expansion will allow us to meet the rapidly growing demand for high-performance 
printed circuit boards in data centers serving artificial-intelligence and 5G applications. 
 
The construction project for the SABIC Fujian petrochemical complex remained on track, reaching 95.3% 
completion. Additionally, we started up a new MTBE project in Saudi Arabia with the capacity to annually 
produce 1 million tons, enhancing the value we capture through downstream integration.  
 
Our innovation program provided market-driven solutions for customers in the form of 148 new product 
introductions in 2025.

SABIC reported revenue of SAR 116.53 billion [US$ 31.07 billion], reflecting a 1% decrease relative to 2024. 
This was primarily driven by lower average selling prices across key products, partially offset by higher 
sales volumes.  
 
Income from operations (EBIT) was SAR 4.37 billion [US$ 1.17 billion]. Adjusted EBIT stood at SAR 6.92 
billion [US$ 1.85 billion]. 
  
EBITDA was SAR 16.43 billion [US$ 4.38 billion], a decrease of 22% compared to 2024. Adjusted EBITDA 
reached SAR 17.88 billion [US$ 4.77 billion], a 15% decrease Y-o-Y, mainly due to squeezed margins. 
  
The 2025 EBITDA margin was 14.1 %. The adjusted EBITDA margin was 15.3%, compared to 17.8% in 2024. 
The drop in adjusted EBITDA margin reflects the effect of lower selling prices amid challenging market 
conditions. 
 
Net income attributable to SABIC shareholders was SAR -25.78 billion [US$ -6.87 billion]. Adjusted net 
income was SAR 2.07 billion [US$ 0.55 billion], compared to SAR 5.88 billion [US$ 1.57 billion] in 2024. The 
net-income loss is the result of deliberate and decisive portfolio actions taken to structurally improve the 
company’s bottom-line performance and position it for long-term value creation.

The Petrochemicals segment covers two SABIC businesses: Chemicals, and Polymers. 
 
Chemicals:  
 
• 
In 2025, the monoethylene glycol market faced significant volatility due to oversupply and weak 
demand. Year-end destocking and subdued restocking activities further contributed to the bearish 
market sentiment.  
• 
Methanol prices faced downward trends throughout the year, reflecting the oversupply and subdued 
demand conditions. 
• 
The methyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE) market experienced normal supply/demand dynamics, with average 
prices fluctuating in response to changes in gasoline prices. In the last quarter of 2025, it went through 
another quarter of price decline as gasoline prices eased, leading to weakened blending margins that 
weighed on MTBE demand. 
 
Polymers: 
 
• 
The global polyethylene (PE) market faced supply/demand imbalances in 2025 that led to lower PE 
prices compared to 2024. 
• 
Polypropylene prices were also lower as a consequence of regional market dynamics and global 
economic factors. 
• 
The polycarbonate market was characterized in 2025 by supply pressures and fluctuating demand.

OUTLOOK: 
 
Looking ahead, SABIC remains focused on long-term value creation through operational excellence, 
portfolio optimization, transformation, and selective growth. The company maintains its disciplined 
approach to capital investment1  with full-year expenditure guidance in the range of US$3.5 to 4 billion.

ABOUT SABIC:  
 
SABIC (TASI: 2010) ranks among the world’s largest petrochemicals manufacturers. It is a public 
company based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. 70% of the company’s shares are owned by Saudi Aramco, with 
the remaining 30% publicly traded on the Saudi stock exchange. SABIC serves customers in more than 
140 countries with a global workforce of more than 28,000 talented individuals. To learn more about 
SABIC please visit www.sabic.com

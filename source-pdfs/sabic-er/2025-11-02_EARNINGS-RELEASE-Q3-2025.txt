Saudi Basic Industries Corporation (“SABIC”) (2010-SA) today announced its financial results for the third 
quarter of 2025, reporting SAR 1.11 billion [US$ 0.30 billion] in adjusted net income for the nine months 
ending on September 30, 2025.  
THIRD QUARTER 2025 HIGHLIGHTS: 
• Revenue of SAR 34.33 billion [US$ 9.16 billion], a decrease of 3.5% Q-o-Q and of 6.9% Y-o-Y.
• Adjusted2 EBITDA1 of SAR 4.99 billion [US$ 1.33 billion], a decrease of 4.5% Q-o-Q and of 10.3% Y-o-Y.
• Adjusted2 EBIT3 of SAR 1.74 billion [US$ 0.46 billion], a decrease of 10.2% Q-o-Q and of 25.8% Y-o-Y.
• Adjusted2 Net Income4 of SAR 0.70 billion [US$ 0.19 billion], an increase of 44.7% Q-o-Q and a
decrease of 39.4% Y-o-Y.
• Adjusted2 Earnings Per Share4 of SAR 0.23 [US$ 0.06], an increase of SAR 0.07 [US$ 0.02] Q-o-Q and a
decrease of SAR 0.15 [US$ 0.04] Y-o-Y.
• Net cash position of SAR 0.61 billion [US$ 0.16 billion] as of September 30, 2025, compared to a net
cash position of SAR 2.71 billion [US$ 0.72 billion] as of June 30, 2025.
ABDULRAHMAN AL-FAGEEH, CEO AND EXECUTIVE BOARD MEMBER: 
“Q3 2025 reflected a moderately improving macroeconomic landscape. Yet, industry overcapacity 
persisted in the petrochemical industry, continuing to squeeze margins and depress utilization rates. 
Amid these conditions, SABIC remained committed to delivering on its 2025 strategic priorities. We 
achieved a Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) of 0.07, reaffirming the importance of health, safety, and 
operational excellence to us. And our adjusted EBITDA of SAR 4.99 [US$ 1.33 billion] showed our resilience. 
We continued to make strong progress on key strategic growth projects. Our MTBE project at Petrokemya 
in Saudi Arabia achieved mechanical completion and has successfully started its operation ahead of 
schedule. In China, the SABIC Fujian complex advanced to 87% Engineering procurement and construction 
(EPC) completion, with over 34 million safe man-hours recorded. Both projects are aligned with our growth 
roadmap and will serve as long-term value drivers. 
Our transformation journey also progressed this quarter, delivering US$ 300 million in realized value, 
bringing us closer to our 2030 goal of US$ 3 billion in recurring EBITDA. On the digital front, we 
successfully launched an upgraded Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) environment, that seamlessly 
integrates our end-to-end. This digital operations platform establishes the foundation for smarter, faster 
and more-connected ways of working. 
This quarter, we introduced 91 new products, tailored to customer needs across global markets. We also 
expanded our low-carbon portfolio, certifying several new products and achieved our first sale of certified 
low-carbon methanol. 
Our commitment to advancing material technologies continues to gain global recognition. Our fire-
retardant, long-glass–filled polypropylene for electric vehicle battery enclosures earned the prestigious 
2025 R&D 100 Award, reinforcing SABIC’s position as a leader in material technology.  
Finally, we remain deeply engaged in supporting Saudi Arabia’s industrial ecosystem. SABIC’s Local 
Content Certificate score reached 56.4%, a 17% year-on-year increase. This achievement highlights our 
efforts to empower industrial entrepreneurship and drive sustainable growth.

SABIC reported revenue of SAR 34.33 billion [US$ 9.16 billion], reflecting a 3.5% decrease relative to Q2 
2025, primarily driven by the absence of licensing revenue which was recorded in Q2. 
Gross profit reached SAR 6.11 billion [US$ 1.63 billion], marking a 38% increase quarter-on-quarter, mainly 
due to a lower cost of sales following the Q2 Teesside impairment impact. 
Free Cash Flow improved to SAR 4.34 billion [US$ 1.16 billion] in Q3, while Free Cash Flow for the nine 
months ended September 30, 2025 amounted to SAR 3.86 billion [US$ 1.03 billion] showing an increase of 
80.6% against the prior year period.  
Income from operations (EBIT) was SAR 1.66 billion [US$ 0.44 billion], reflecting a strong quarter-on-
quarter increase, largely due to the absence of impairments and provisions recorded in Q2. 
Adjusted EBIT stood at SAR 1.74 billion [US$ 0.47 billion]. 
EBITDA was SAR 4.92 billion [US$ 1.31 billion], flat versus the previous quarter. Adjusted EBITDA reached 
SAR 4.99 billion [US$ 1.33 billion], a 4.5% decrease Q-o-Q due to the absence of licensing revenue  in Q3.  
The EBITDA margin rose slightly to 14.3%, while the adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 14.5%, compared to 
13.8% and 14.7%, respectively, in Q2 2025.

The Petrochemicals segment covers two SABIC businesses: Chemicals, and Polymers. 
Chemicals: 
•
Mono ethylene glycol (MEG) prices remained low as oversupply persisted, particularly from new
capacity.
•
Methanol prices declined in Q3 due to weak demand from derivative markets.
•
Mthyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE) prices were flat on ample supply driven by new start-ups, and a
weaker-than-usual summer driving season.
Polymers: 
•
Polyethylene (PE) prices declined, pressured by persistent oversupply and sluggish global
demand.
•
Polypropylene (PP) prices were lower on ample supply and high inventories.
•
Polycarbonate (PC) prices declined in all key regions on ample supply.

OUTLOOK: 
Looking ahead, SABIC remains focused on long-term value creation through operational excellence, 
transformation, and selective growth. The company maintains its disciplined approach to capital 
investment1  with full-year expenditure guidance in the range of US$3.0 to 3.5 billion. 
1 Capital expenditure (“CAPEX”) and capital injection for SABIC’s growth project.

ABOUT SABIC: 
SABIC (TASI: 2010) ranks among the world’s largest petrochemicals manufacturers. It is a public 
company based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. 70% of the Company’s shares are owned by Saudi Aramco, with 
the remaining 30% publicly traded on the Saudi stock exchange. SABIC serves customers in more than 
140 countries with a global workforce of more than 28,000 talented individuals. In 2024, its overall 
production was 53.9 million metric tons; its total assets were US$ 74.1 billion, and its net income from 
continuing operations was US$ 0.6 billion. To learn more about SABIC please visit www.sabic.com 
For further information, please contact SABIC Investor Relations at IR@SABIC.com or visit 
www.sabic.com/en/investors

The Saudi Basic Industries Corporation (“SABIC”) (2010-SA) today announced 
its financial results for the third quarter of 2026, reporting a 14% increase in 
revenue, and a 40% increase in Adjusted EBITDA Q-o-Q. 
 
Third quarter 2026 highlights: 
• Revenue of SAR 28.17 billion [US$ 7.51 billion], an increase of 14% Q-o-Q. 
• Adjusted2 EBITDA1 of SAR 4.75 billion [US$ 1.27 billion], an increase of 40% Q-o-Q. 
• Adjusted2 EBIT3 of SAR 2.17 billion [US$ 0.58 billion], an increase of 434% Q-o-Q. 
• Adjusted2 Net (Loss) Income4 of SAR 0.84 billion [US$ 0.22 billion] compared to an adjusted Net (loss) of 
SAR (0.38) billion [US$ (0.1) billion] in Q2. 
• Adjusted2 Earnings Per Share4 of SAR 0.28 [US$ 0.07] Compared to an adjusted Earnings Per Share of SAR 
(0.13) [US$ (0.03)]. 
• Net debt position of SAR 3.03 billion [US$ 0.81 billion] as of September 30, 2026, compared to a net debt 
position of SAR 2.73 billion [US$ 0.73 billion] as of June 30, 2026.

Dr. Faisal ALFAQEER, CEO and Executive Board Member:  
“In 3Q2026, SABIC demonstrated exceptional resilience, despite geopolitical developments and market 
headwinds, coupled with a clear and robust forward-looking vision and continued focus on strategic 
objectives, namely portfolio optimization, transformation and selective growth, in efforts to support the 
company’s long-term profitability and value creation for shareholders, building on its historic journey of 
more than five decades. 
 
Quarterly key highlights included achieving an exceptional Total Recordable Incident Rate of 0.06, 
demonstrating our prioritization of safety and security; SABIC Agri-Nutrients’ Final Investment Decision to 
build a new industrial complex for ammonia and urea production in the Kingdom, with total annual 
production capacity of 1.2 million metric tons for ammonia and 2.6 million metric tons per annum for urea, 
increasing the company’s urea’s production capacity by 54% compared to current levels; the allocation of 
necessary feedstock by the Ministry of Energy for a new methanol production plant in Jubail, Kingdom of 
Saudi Arabia with a production capacity of 1.8 million tons per year, propelling our growth plans in strategic 
profitable markets; the completion of the divestment of the Engineering Thermoplastics business in the 
Americas and Europe for an enterprise value of $450 million, as part of our Portfolio Optimization Program; 
taking further steps in our transformation journey, whereby $0.95 billion (SAR 3.56 billion) was so far 
achieved of the 2030 target of $3 billion (SAR 11.25 billion) as a positive recurring impact on the EBITDA 
level; and receiving a Local Content Certificate for 2025 from the Local Content and Government 
Procurement Authority, as SABIC achieved over 61% as Local Content Score in 2025, an increase of more 
than 9% compared to 2024, an accomplishment that was further supported by concluding several 
agreements focused on local industries’ enablement, such as the recently signed MoU with Lenovo.”

SABIC reported revenue of SAR 28.17 billion [US$ 7.51 billion] in Q3 2026, reflecting a 14% increase 
compared to Q2 2026. This increase was primarily driven by 30% higher sales volume Q-o-Q as seen in a 24% 
increase in Chemicals sales, 23% increase in Polymers, and an increase in Agri Nutrients sales by 68%. Sales 
volume increases across the board were the results of SABIC’s effort to navigate logistical challenges through 
successful shipments via Strait of Hormuz and west ports utilization. The sales volume increase was partially 
offset by lower average selling prices across some key products as shown in the segments part. 
  
Despite the increase in the logistical cost, EBITDA was SAR 4.74 billion [US$ 1.26 billion], an increase of 25% 
compared to Q2 2026. On an adjusted basis, EBITDA increased by 40% Q-o-Q, mainly due to higher sales 
volume and improved margins as a result of lower liquid feedstock costs. 
 
The EBITDA margin was 16.81%, while the adjusted EBITDA margin reached 16.87%, up from 13.64% in Q2 
2026. The increase in adjusted EBITDA margin reflects the higher sales and improved margins which were 
mainly due to reduction in liquid feedstock costs. 
 
Net Income from Continuing Operations was SAR 2.53 billion [US$ 676 million], on an adjusted basis 
however, the net income was SAR 843.76 million [US$ 225 million]. The difference is mainly attributed to 
reversal of impairment in Clariant as a result of its share price appreciation. 
Free cash flow of SAR 5.61 billion [US$ 1.5 billion] YTD 2026. An increase of 2% compared with same period 
last year due to optimized CAPEX spending as part of SABIC’s broader transformation efforts.

The Petrochemicals segment covers two SABIC businesses: Chemicals, and Polymers. 
 
o 
Total Petrochemicals sales volume of 6.71 MMT during Q3 2026 an increase of 24% compared to last 
quarter. Chemicals sales volume was 3.67 MMT higher by 24%, and Polymers sales volume was 2.95 
MMT which was 23% higher Q-o-Q. 
Chemicals:  
 
• 
In Q3 2026, monoethylene glycol prices increased supported by historically low inventories in Asia 
caused by continued shipping disruptions from the Middle East. Demand was on the lower side, with low 
polyester Operating Rates in Asia, decrease of North America demand after world cup, and Europe 
remained weak with ample PET import. 
 
• 
Methanol (MEOH) prices lowered from high Q2 on lower upstream and weaker Asian demand. US and 
Europe followed lower Asian trend. Lower Asian demand on poor methanol-to-olefin margins and weak 
formaldehyde production was offset by better demand in West on higher AA and MTBE production in US 
and fuel related demand in Europe. 
 
• 
Methyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE) prices strengthened on higher gasoline values from better demand in 
both Asia and Europe due to summer driving season and favorable blending economics.  
 
Polymers: 
 
• 
In Q3 2026, polyethylene prices lowered across regions from high Q2 with anticipated easing of SoH 
early in the quarter. Supply up globally helped by export from US and improved local production is Asia. 
Demand was up in Asia up on lower prices.  
 
• 
Polypropylene prices lower across regions from high Q2 due to lower upstream costs. Stable to lower 
demand in key regions and buying sentiment remains cautious.  
 
• 
Polycarbonate prices saw mixed trends; Asian prices lowered on weaker upstream and supply increase. 
US prices stable, and Europe prices pressured on additional imports. Demand remained generally weak 
and buyers stayed cautious amid macro and geopolitical uncertainty.

OUTLOOK: 
Looking ahead, SABIC aims to continue focusing on sustainable long-term value creation through 
operational excellence, portfolio optimization, corporate transformation, and selective growth. 
The company maintains its disciplined approach to capital investment1  with full-year expenditure guidance 
in the range of US$ 3 to 3.3 billion. 
1 Capital expenditure (“CAPEX”) and capital injection for SABIC’s growth project, including CAPEX expensed off in the discontinued businesses.

About SABIC:  
SABIC (TASI: 2010) ranks among the world’s largest petrochemicals manufacturers. It is a public company 
based in Riyadh, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. 70% of the company’s shares are owned by Aramco 
Downstream Company a wholly owned subsidiary of Saudi Aramco, with the remaining 30% publicly traded 
on the Saudi Exchange. SABIC serves customers in more than 140 countries with a global workforce of more 
than 26,000 talented individuals. To learn more about SABIC please visit www.sabic.com
